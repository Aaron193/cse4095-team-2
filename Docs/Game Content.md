# Daily Delve: Game Content

_Generated by `npm run docs` from the engine data. Do not edit by hand._

## How a run works

1. **Briefing.** Everyone sees the same dungeon for the day, fully revealed. You are offered 9 cards (3 Fire, 3 Frost, 3 Poison) and pick 4.
2. **Delve.** Move one tile at a time. Every move, wait or card is one turn; enemies and traps then take theirs. Nothing is random.
3. **Score.** Reach the stairs. Fewer moves, less time, more health and unused charges all score higher.

You have 10 HP and a dagger: walking into an enemy deals 1 damage. Cards have limited charges and a card that would do nothing is never spent. Most obstacles have more than one answer (a card, a crate, a detour, good timing), and every published dungeon is bot-verified to have at least two different winning card sets.

## Score formula

| Part | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Clear | 1000 for reaching the exit; otherwise up to 600 for how close you got |
| Moves | 1000 x min(1.2, par / your moves) |
| Time | 500 at 3:00 or faster, falling evenly to 0 at 12:00 |
| Health | 40 per HP left |
| Thrift | 20 per unused card charge (max 200) |
| Gold | 25 per gold (max 300) |
| Style | 60 per combo or trick kill, 15 per kill (max 300) |

Moves, Time, Health and Thrift only count if you clear. **Par** is the best run the solver bot found. Leaderboard order: cleared, then score, then fewer moves, then earlier submission. Everything except Time is re-simulated from the replay code.
